About us

Learn about PixPDFKit and our approach to free online PDF tools.

What is PixPDFKit?

PixPDFKit is an independent project focused on straightforward PDF utilities. It helps students, professionals and small businesses combine, separate and transform documents without needing an account or uploading files to a server.

How do the tools work?

Most features use established JavaScript libraries inside your browser. Document contents are handled locally. Your browser may still retrieve application code and fonts from third-party content delivery services.

Clear limitations

We do not promise every PDF will work. Password-protected or damaged documents can fail, large files may exhaust device memory, and raster-based compression can remove searchable text. Always keep a copy of the original document.

Improvements and feedback

Please report issues and suggest improvements using our contact page. We are not affiliated with Adobe, Google or any other PDF provider.
