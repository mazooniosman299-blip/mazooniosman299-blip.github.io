Privacy policy

Learn how PixPDFKit handles documents and browsing data.

1. Document processing

PixPDFKit PDF tools run in your browser. Our application code does not send document contents to our own server. Your original documents remain on your device and results are offered as browser downloads.

2. Hosting and external libraries

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose provider may keep technical access logs subject to its policies. Your browser fetches certain libraries from jsDelivr and cdnjs, and fonts from Google Fonts. These providers can receive network request details, including IP addresses, but this site's application code does not transmit your document contents to them.

3. Cookies and advertising

At present, this site has no Google AdSense advertising code or behavioural analytics and does not create marketing cookies. If advertising is activated later, we will update this policy and implement the notices and consent mechanisms applicable in relevant regions. Google may then use cookies or advertising identifiers subject to its policies.

4. Contact information

If you email us, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to respond or improve the service. Avoid including confidential documents or sensitive personal data.

5. Your choices

Contact us to ask about or request deletion of emails you sent. Last updated: October 28, 2026. Material changes to practices will be reflected on this page.
