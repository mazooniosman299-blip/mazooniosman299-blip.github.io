When to use this tool

A watermark marks draft copies and internal review documents with visible text such as DRAFT or INTERNAL. The label is drawn diagonally with transparency over every page of the selected PDF.

How to use it

Choose your PDF, enter the watermark text, and adjust opacity. Processing creates watermarked.pdf with the label near the center of each page. Keep the text short because the current tool uses only its first 35 characters.

Limits and cautions

A watermark is a visible marking, not encryption, copy prevention, or protection against editing. Non-Latin scripts can render differently depending on browser fonts, and high opacity can obscure details.

Check before sharing

Review a page with tables and another with signatures to ensure numbers remain legible. Do not treat the watermark as a substitute for controlling who receives a sensitive document.

🔒 Your files stay private

Files are processed on your device with JavaScript and are not uploaded to our servers. An internet connection is needed to load certain libraries.
