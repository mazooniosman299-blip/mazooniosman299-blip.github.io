When to use it

Images to PDF combines phone photographs of certificates, receipts, and scanned forms in one document. It accepts JPG, PNG, and WebP files and turns each selected image into a PDF page in the displayed file order.

Steps to follow

Add images, use the up/down controls beside their names to order the pages, and select A4 or the available image-derived page size. Download images-to-pdf.pdf and open it to verify the first page, last page, and orientation of the photos.

Limits and cautions

Images are internally converted to JPEG on a white background, so transparent PNG or WebP areas become white and some fine details may change. Up to 40 images of 80 MB each may be queued, subject to browser-memory limits.

Verify the output

In A4 mode, images are fitted inside the paper without deliberate cropping, which can leave white margins. Check every edge of a photographed document before sharing, and avoid relying on blurred or low-resolution photos for official submissions.

🔒 Your files stay private

Files are processed on your device with JavaScript and are not uploaded to our servers. An internet connection is needed to load certain libraries.
