When to use this tool

This tool produces a new PDF without selected blank, duplicate, or unwanted pages. It is suitable for cleaning a document before sending it and never deletes the original saved on your device.

How to use it

Select one PDF and type the pages to remove, such as 2,5-7. Remaining pages keep their original order in pages-removed.pdf. Check the numbers before processing because the field lists pages to delete, not pages to retain.

Limits and trade-offs

The tool refuses to remove every page; at least one page must remain. Encrypted or damaged PDFs may not open, and digital signatures or interactive form fields can change when a new PDF is written.

Check the output

Removing a page is not secure redaction of hidden metadata or embedded attachments. Inspect every resulting page, and use a dedicated document-redaction tool when sensitive information needs to be permanently removed.

🔒 Your files stay private

Files are processed on your device with JavaScript and are not uploaded to our servers. An internet connection is needed to load certain libraries.
