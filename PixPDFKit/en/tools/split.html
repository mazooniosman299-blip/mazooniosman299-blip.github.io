How it works

  1. Select files from your device, or drag them to the upload area.
  2. Adjust the options you need, then select Process files.
  3. When processing is finished, select Download result to save your output.

What to know about this tool

Choose the page range you want and save it as a separate PDF document. Your documents are opened in the browser and the resulting file is prepared for download without requiring an account.

This tool supports unencrypted PDF documents. Interactive fields and digital signatures may not survive editing.

Common question

Will splitting change my original file?

No. The operation creates a new file in your browser and leaves the original unchanged.

🔒 Your files stay private

Files are processed on your device with JavaScript and are not uploaded to our servers. An internet connection is needed to load certain libraries.
